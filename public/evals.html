Stocker agent evals

Each case sends a made-up home and a message to the live agent in dry-run mode, then checks the result with fixed rules. Nothing in your real home is read or changed. You must be signed in to Stocker in this browser.

Cost: a full run makes one or two model calls per case, about 60 in all, plus one for each test made from your conversations. On Cloudflare's free plan that is a large share of the daily AI allowance, which the app itself also needs, so run it after a change rather than routinely.

Learning from real conversations

Every chat turn is kept with private details removed. The ones below went wrong: someone tapped "Not right?", undid a change, rejected a guess, corrected me, had to say it twice, or named something I could not find. Say what should have happened and it becomes a test I must pass from then on. Nothing typed in a chat changes how I behave by itself.
